This can be used to monitor BPE-processes.
Basically, once it is correctly set-up, you can just enter .RUN files and status-messages are sent via telegram to your phone.
You need the following things:
- error_watch_manual.txt
- error_watch.sh
- IGNORE.LIST
- WATCH.LIST

A guide how to set-up the telegram notification can be found in the manual at the very bottom. Please do not just copy and run, but set-up the monitor correctly. Otherwise CK will get all the messages.
